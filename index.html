    What Hello can do for you

    Ask in plain words, or tap a suggestion. Hello works across your whole CRM, not just one screen.

    Tells you what to do next

    A ranked Next steps list: overdue tasks, leads going cold, hot leads, new inquiries nobody has contacted, tasks due today and open cases.

    Writes the email for you

    Reads the lead's message, your past emails, tasks and notes, then drafts a follow-up. Edit the subject and message, then send.

    Adds records by chat

    Say "Add a lead: Anna Cruz, wants a quote". Hello fills in leads, clients, tasks, cases, deals and case notes.

    Moves your pipeline

    Moves deals between stages, updates an inquiry's status, logs that you contacted someone and sets schedules.

    Handles inbox and team chat

    "Reply to my latest email" or post an update in a client's team chat. Hello drafts it and you confirm.

    Plans your day

    Summarizes what is open, which cases and deals are at risk, and ends with the single best next step.

    One-tap shortcuts

    Mark it done, push it to tomorrow, "I already contacted them", or add a follow-up task, all without typing.

    Knows where you are

    Suggestions change with the page you are on: Inquiries, Tasks, Cases, Deals, Inbox and more.

    Just say it your way

    Clients, tasks and cases in one home
