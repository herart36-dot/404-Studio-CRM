    Orchid CRM
    Overview

    Know who is waiting, who has gone quiet, and what comes next.

    Orchid CRM is a simple home for every inquiry, client and task. It is built for agencies, clinics, service teams, support desks and small sales teams that want to respond faster and follow up better, without a sales call or a per-seat price maze.

    What Orchid CRM does

    1

    Inquiries with a live waiting timer

    Every new inquiry shows how long it has been waiting.

    The longest-waiting client is always visible, so replies do not slip.
    2

    Going-cold alerts

    Clients who have been quiet for five or more days are flagged.

    You follow up before the relationship fades.
    3

    Cases with routing

    Each case is sent to the right person on your team.

    Every issue has a clear owner.
    4

    SLA tracking

    Cases are tracked against your response targets.

    Risk shows early instead of arriving as a complaint.
    5

    Team chat log for every client

    Every conversation with a client is kept in one log.

    Anyone can pick up where a colleague left off.
    6

    Clients, tasks and cases in one home

    Clients, what you owe them and what they asked for sit together.

    No jumping between inboxes and spreadsheets.
