Orchid CRM
OrchidCRM
One calm home for every inquiry, client and task.

For small teams who handle requests from several places and need to know who is waiting and who is going quiet.

What it does

  • Inquiries: new, contacted, quoted, won, lost, with a live "waiting" timer.
  • Going-cold alerts when a lead has been quiet for 5+ days.
  • Clients with contact details, notes and history.
  • Tasks with due dates, priorities and overdue highlights.
  • Cases: turn emails, chats and calls into numbered tickets.
  • Routing and SLA: rules assign owners; missed deadlines escalate.
  • Team chat log for every client, with unread badges.

Why teams like it

  • A "Do this next" card names the single best action right now.
  • Average first-reply time shows how fast you really respond.
  • Approved members only; your data stays with your team.
  • Works well on phones, with nothing to learn.

Orchid Pals
OrchidPals
Share a moment. Say it in your own words. Get along.

A private, members-only community. Members post stories with photos or voice notes, react, add each other as Pals and chat one to one. Your own social space, without the noise or the strangers.

What members can do

  • Post stories and attach a photo.
  • Voice notes: record and share a voice story.
  • React to what others share.
  • Pals: search people, send requests, build a circle.
  • Private chat with your Pals.
  • Notifications for new stories, messages and requests.

Built for organizations

  • Invite only: only admin-approved emails can join.
  • A calm place for your members to connect, away from public feeds.
  • One login with the rest of Orchid.

Orchid Solopreneur
OrchidSolopreneur
Know who to reply to, what to do today, and who owes you money.

For one-person and local businesses: salons, repair shops, tutors, caterers, cleaners, photographers and freelancers. Just the essentials.

What it does

  • Today: a "Do this next" card with money owed first.
  • Leads: a pipeline with estimated value and one-tap call, text or email.
  • Customers: history, total paid, last visit and a "Book job" button.
  • Jobs and payments: date, price, done, paid or unpaid.
  • Time to check in: reminds you of customers you have not seen in 60 days.
  • Tasks with due dates.

Why it fits small business

  • See earned this month and waiting to be paid at a glance.
  • Repeat customers are local income, and it nudges you to bring them back.
  • Each business sees only its own data.
