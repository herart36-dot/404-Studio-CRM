Button guide

What every button and tab in Orchid does.

Top bar

+ New lead
Opens a form to add a lead by hand (calls, walk-ins, referrals).
⌘K
Command palette. Jump to any tab or lead by typing. Shortcut: Ctrl/⌘ + K.
✎ Message
Write to a lead directly: pick the lead, type your message, then send via Gmail, your email app, or Reddit. Also available as the ✎ button inside any lead.
Profile (round photo, top right)
Opens your profile card. Click Edit profile to change your photo, name, title, phone, location and company, then Save.
Refresh
Reloads the latest leads, emails and tasks from the database.
Sign out
Logs you out of this device.
Client site
Opens the client-facing website in a new tab (shown when available).
Search all leads
Searches every lead by name, email or service from any tab.

Tabs

Home
Daily summary: key numbers and what needs attention today.
Leads
All enquiries as a table. Click a row to open the lead and edit it.
Inbox
Emails sent to your business address, matched to leads by email.
Pipeline
Kanban board. Drag a card to another column to change its stage.
Calendar
Booked calls and meetings, plus follow-ups (overdue ones in red).
Tasks
To-dos linked to leads. Tick to complete.
Forecast
Open deals weighted by how likely each stage is to close.
Websites
Tracks each client site from request to launch.
Chat
Live chat with your student: a General room and private messages.
Reports
Charts and totals. Use the period dropdown to change the date range.
Members
Everyone in your organization. Add a student by their email, message them, edit their profile and photo, or remove them.

Leads tab

Stat boxes
Click a status count (New, Contacted…) to filter the table. Click again to clear.
Date received
Limits leads to today, last 7/30 days, this month or a custom range.
Sort
Newest first, oldest first, or soonest appointment.
Export CSV
Downloads the leads currently shown as a spreadsheet file.
Clear filters
Appears when a filter is on. Resets the list to everything.
← Back
On phones, returns from a lead to the list.

Inside a lead

Stage path (arrows)
Click a stage to move the lead forward or mark it lost.
Status, appointment, follow-up
Fields you set on the lead. Save to keep changes.
Save / Delete
Save stores your notes and changes. Delete removes the lead permanently.
