Meet the first one
OrchidCRM
One calm home for every inquiry, client and task.

For small teams who handle requests from several places and need to know who is waiting and who is going quiet.

What it does

  • Inquiries: new, contacted, quoted, won, lost, with a live "waiting" timer.
  • Going-cold alerts when a lead has been quiet for 5+ days.
  • Clients with contact details, notes and history.
  • Tasks with due dates, priorities and overdue highlights.
  • Cases: turn emails, chats and calls into numbered tickets.
  • Routing and SLA: rules assign owners; missed deadlines escalate.
  • Team chat log per client with unread badges.

Why people choose it

  • A "Do this next" card names the single best action right now.
  • Average first-reply time shows how fast you really respond.
  • Approved members only; data stays with your team.
  • Works well on phones; nothing to learn.

OrchidPals
Share a moment. Say it in your own words. Get along.

A private, members-only community. People post stories with photos or voice notes, react, add each other as Pals and chat one to one. Your own social space, without the noise or the strangers.

What members can do

  • Post stories and attach a photo (up to 8 MB).
  • Voice notes: record and share a voice story.
  • React to posts.
  • Pals: search people, send requests, build a circle.
  • Private chat with your Pals.
  • Notifications for stories, messages and requests.

For organizations

  • Invite only: just admin-approved emails can join.
  • Clubs, schools, alumni, churches, neighborhoods, company culture.

OrchidSolopreneur
Know who to reply to, what to do today, and who owes you money.

For one-person and local businesses: salons, repair shops, tutors, caterers, cleaners, photographers and freelancers. Just the essentials.

What it does

  • Today: a "Do this next" card with money owed first.
  • Leads: pipeline with estimated value and one-tap call, text or email.
  • Customers: history, total paid, last visit, "Book job".
  • Jobs and payments: date, price, done, paid or unpaid.
  • Time to check in: reminds you of customers unseen for 60 days.
  • Tasks with due dates.

Marketing guide

How to position and promote each app

Lead with the problem each audience feels today, then show the calm way out.

Sample taglines

CRM: Never lose a lead to a slow reply.
Pals: Your community, minus the noise.
Solopreneur: Your business in your pocket.

Channels that work

  • Facebook and Messenger groups where local owners already are.
  • Short video: a 20-second "my day in Solopreneur".
  • Community admins: offer Pals free to a first group for testimonials.
  • Referrals: each happy owner introduces one more business.
  • Local partners: co-working spaces, small business associations.

Launch plan

  • Week 1: onboard 5 friendly businesses and 1 community.
  • Weeks 2 to 3: collect feedback and 3 testimonials.
  • Week 4: publish case stories and open a waiting list.
  • Measure: sign-ups, weekly active users, jobs logged, posts per member.

Trust message

  • Approved-members-only sign-up
  • Business data is private to each user
  • One login, no scattered passwords
  • Photos are stored privately and shown to members only
